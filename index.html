About Us

Welcome to
Brother Kick

Located in the heart of Shah Alam, Brother Kick Muay Thai is more than just a gym — it's a community. Whether you're a complete beginner or a seasoned fighter, you'll find a home here.

Our experienced coaches are dedicated to developing not just your technique, but your character. Step in, gloves on, and become part of the family.
